Cricket

Cricket is a bat-and-ball game played between two teams of eleven players each. It originated in south-east England in the 16th century and spread across the British Empire, becoming hugely popular in countries such as India, Australia, England, Pakistan, South Africa, New Zealand, Sri Lanka and the West Indies.

The game is played on a large oval field with a 22-yard rectangular strip in the centre called the pitch. At each end of the pitch stand the wickets, made of three wooden stumps topped by two bails. One team bats and tries to score runs, while the other team bowls and fields, trying to dismiss the batters and limit the runs scored.

A batter can be dismissed in several ways, including being bowled, caught, leg before wicket (LBW), run out or stumped. Runs are scored by running between the wickets or by hitting the ball to the boundary, which earns four runs, or six if it clears the boundary without bouncing.

Cricket is played in three main formats: Test matches, which last up to five days; One Day Internationals (ODIs), with 50 overs per side; and Twenty20 (T20), with 20 overs per side. The ICC Cricket World Cup and the Indian Premier League (IPL) are among the most watched cricket events in the world.

Legendary players such as Sachin Tendulkar, Don Bradman and Virat Kohli have inspired millions of fans worldwide.
